## Corrected blockers

Wikimedia now advertises thumbnails on `thumb.wikimedia.org`; the portrait adapters only allowed `upload.wikimedia.org` and `commons.wikimedia.org`. Six of eleven inspected cached portrait candidates used the rejected host. The [official migration announcement](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Tech/News/2026/36) confirms the new domain. A shared exact-host policy now supports it, retains redirect/private-address/lookalike checks, and reopens affected queue failures through a policy version change. Existing evidence, cached HTTP responses and image files remain intact. The corrected path saved Śāntideva's credited depiction in a live batch.

[MediaWiki maxlag responses](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Manual:Maxlag_parameter) were also treated as generic 15-minute outages. HTTP 200 maxlag now honors `Retry-After`, with a five-second minimum/default and bounded exponential recovery up to five minutes. Longer server waits remain authoritative. HTTP 403/429 and unknown outages retain their longer backoff. Existing longer cooldowns are preserved. The safe reason, HTTP status and outage type survive host/provider persistence. Legacy cached API errors are treated as cache misses after the applicable cooldown instead of being replayed indefinitely.

Image audit records now distinguish zero discovered candidates from actual download rejection reasons. A future host-policy rejection is visible instead of disappearing into an unexplained `no_safe_match`.

## Added sources

- **Wellcome Collection** (`wellcome-portraits`): the [public catalogue API](https://developers.wellcomecollection.org/docs/catalogue) and IIIF images. At most three metadata requests per person. A matching catalog book's author authority must equal the portrait subject's authority; a name alone is insufficient. Group portraits, books/letters, non-portrait scenes and unsupported rights are excluded. Per-image licence, artist, credit and identity evidence are retained. A live read-only check found and decoded the public-domain Mungo Park portrait, linked to *Travels in the Interior Districts of Africa*. Its first ingestion attempt deferred to a recorded connection cooldown; **a database save from this provider has not yet been verified**.
- **Nobel Prize** (`nobel-portraits`): the [keyless laureate API](https://www.nobelprize.org/organization/developer-zone-2/) plus official facts/bibliography pages. The bulk name index is parsed once per worker process; non-laureates do not cause individual web requests. Exact names and catalog-work corroboration are required. Only modern photographs explicitly credited to Nobel copyright holders are accepted under their [stated portrait-use terms](https://www.nobelprize.org/about/copyright-information/#portraits), with the noncommercial editorial/educational restriction retained. Older and third-party portraits are excluded. Herta Müller and László Krasznahorkai were actually saved. This source has a small current opportunity; it is not a route to thousands of portraits.

Poetry Foundation and Poets.org were evaluated but not automated because their published terms restrict scraping. No generic website crawler, generated likenesses, relaxed identity matching or rate-limit bypass was introduced.

DBpedia batches up to ten people into one identity request and one Commons metadata request. It requires a human identity and an authored catalog work, or the established shared-ranked philosopher exception. Only its primary thumbnail is considered; ordinary depiction links included unrelated buildings and people. Commons artist/rights/subject metadata is rechecked, with explicit rejection of observed plaque, grave, cover, group and coat-of-arms cases. HTTP200 partial SPARQL results and limit overflow are deferred, never interpreted as complete identity evidence. Header-verified response caches are distinct from older unchecked responses.

GND requires a complete authority search and exact author role, name, catalog title and authority-ID agreement. Bibliography pagination is unnecessary when one authority remains and a returned record proves authorship; if several namesakes remain, incomplete bibliography results cannot select one. Its image-specific rights and creator fields are mandatory. [lobid's usage policy](https://lobid.org/usage-policy/) is enforced through shared 2.1-second request spacing and daily caching.

Rijksmuseum requires a museum portrait title, exactly one depicted person with the independently corroborated QID, reciprocal object/visual/digital links, image-specific public-domain rights, and a downloadable full-image reproduction. Its [IIIF endpoint](https://data.rijksmuseum.nl/docs/iiif/image) provides 600-pixel images. A Walpole coat of arms and multi-person scenes were explicitly rejected. The no-work philosopher flag is retained for the existing transactional ranking recheck. DBpedia and Rijksmuseum share identity discovery; DBpedia/GND still share Wikimedia image storage. These are three lookup paths, not three wholly independent infrastructure stacks.
