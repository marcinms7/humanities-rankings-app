# Two provisional ranking lenses — method v1

Version: 12 September 2026. Owner direction: **“Show both orderings to expose the tradeoffs.”**

The deliverable is five pilot comparisons, each ordering the same 25 selected candidates twice. These are concrete editorial ranking proposals. They are **not** completed global Top 25s: the intended targets contain 100–200 entries, and many plausible contenders have not yet received adequate comparisons. A missing title or philosopher has no implied bottom position.

## What the two columns mean

**Critical standing and enduring influence:** a proposed ordering emphasizing sustained scholarly and critical engagement, intellectual or artistic contribution, and reception beyond a single list. Historical importance is not endorsement of a work’s claims or a substitute for quality. The current draft has not quantified how these considerations trade off.

**Reading value today:** a proposed ordering emphasizing insight and the experience of engaging with a work now. Preparation, sustained attention and interpretive difficulty can affect priority. This is not a shortest-books list, a beginner syllabus, or a claim about the owner’s personal tastes. The pilot assumes an interested general reader; that assumption needs refinement. For philosophers, it means the prospective value of engaging with their thought through suitable works, not an average of their books’ positions.

The owner authorized comparing these emphases. They have not approved a final criterion model, weights, assessment values or final personalized positions. All such fields remain null. The ordering is an explicit qualitative proposal, not the output of the scoring engine.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Each candidate has target-specific source IDs, two proposed arguments and an unresolved issue. The sources can establish inclusion, interpretation, reception or disagreement; **they do not establish the proposed exact positions**. Confidence in exact position is low throughout. A one-source dossier is especially weak; an abstract, profile or general selection cannot replace a full work-level comparison. Do not upgrade confidence simply because the target has many sources.

This pilot is not a numerical meta-ranking. No recommendation-frequency score, averaged publisher rank, rating conversion or invented weighting has been used. Nearby positions should be treated as readily reversible. Larger movements between columns expose assumptions that subsequent research should test, not measured differences in quality.

Modern Library’s first position for Ulysses belongs to its own scoped list. OCLC holdings reflect library collections. Penguin’s numbered classics selection explicitly declares itself unordered. Curricula and reading sequences do not supply merit positions. All remain separate from the proposed orderings. Reprints, hosted copies and translated reports of one underlying judgment must not become additional votes.

## Scope and editions

All-books allows complete book-length works across subjects; its pilot includes one complete epic. Literary fiction includes novels and a complete story collection, with no individual stories, poems or plays inserted. Nonfiction includes philosophical argument, testimony and history, but currently overrepresents philosophy. Philosophy books can include complete verse treatises; a fragment, anthology or commentary must not be silently substituted for the named work. Received compilations and multi-volume sequences have explicit identity caveats.

Book positions remain conditional on verification of a specific complete English edition. An English title or a translated review is not verification. The Darwin first-edition transcription is specifically verified; the proposal data does not claim the other recommended editions have been checked. Original English works still require edition records. Attribution disputes are preserved. Persons do not have an edition, but a reading recommendation for them ultimately needs a verified entry work.

## What is saved, and what remains

The cumulative consulted-source ledgers are imported into the existing database. The draft orderings are saved separately in Markdown and versioned JSON. No catalog items, ranking entries, final assessments or published results have been created by this proposal. No completed-research date has been advanced.

Next: expand the candidate universe; strengthen the weakest comparative dossiers with diverse direct criticism and reader evidence; verify English editions and work identities; test contested placements under both emphases; then develop the owner-controlled criterion model. Preserve v1 when revising. Publication is a separate concrete step after evidence and methodology are ready, not an automatic consequence of meeting a source threshold.
