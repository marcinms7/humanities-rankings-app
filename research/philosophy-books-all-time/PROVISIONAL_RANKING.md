## What changes, and why

- Hume’s Enquiry moves from 24 to 1; his Treatise moves from 8 to 22. The source explicitly distinguishes the more concise later work from the larger earlier system.
- Epistemic Injustice moves from 23 to 11: potential conceptual usefulness rises, while the conflicting reviews remain visible.
- Kant moves from 2 to 25 in this scenario. This is a preparation-sensitive reading hypothesis, not a finding of lesser philosophical quality.

These movements are hypotheses made explicit for review. The current evidence does not establish that adjacent candidates are distinguishable with this precision.

## Outstanding comparisons

Avicenna, Aquinas, Śaṅkara, Rāmānuja, Dharmakīrti, Mozi, Mulla Sadra, Kyoto-school works, African philosophy and Latin American philosophy need more work-level comparisons. Further major challengers include Hobbes, Locke, Rousseau, Hegel, Kierkegaard, Marx, Heidegger, Weil, Murdoch, Parfit and noncanonical women. Verse treatises and collected texts require complete-work and translation checks.
