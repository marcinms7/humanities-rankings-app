## What changes, and why

- Proust moves from 2 to 20: the recorded disagreement between immersion and abandonment matters much more in the reading scenario.
- Ulysses moves from 5 to 25: a first-place Modern Library result remains intact as source evidence, while this draft tests a different reading priority.
- Season of Migration to the North moves from 13 to 3. Arabic criticism and rereading testimony help justify investigating this rise; they do not establish a consensus third place.

These movements are hypotheses made explicit for review. The current evidence does not establish that adjacent candidates are distinguishable with this precision.

## Outstanding comparisons

Major challengers needing deeper comparison include Tolstoy’s Anna Karenina, Dostoevsky, Kafka, Flaubert, Woolf, Borges collections, Manzoni, La Regenta, Indonesian works beyond Pramoedya, South Asian language traditions, Korean and Chinese modern fiction. Existing leads include Godaan, Tamas, Toji, Border Town and Love in a Fallen City. The pool is deliberately provisional; unselected candidates have not been defeated in a global comparison.

Before publication: expand the candidate pool, strengthen thin dossiers, verify specific complete English editions, review work/collection identities, and develop the owner-controlled criteria and weights. Keep these draft positions separate from every publisher’s original order and from personal overrides.
