# Placement review after batch 26

The earlier v1 orders remain preserved. The following are proposed revisions to the reasoning, not an automatic six-source reranking.

- **The Magic Mountain:** v1 placed it 24th for reading value primarily because it is demanding. R179 supplies a direct counterexample: initial difficulty preceded exceptional intellectual reward. Reading value must consider payoff after sustained engagement, not treat difficulty as a disqualifier. Reassess within the upper literary tier after comparing complete editions and wider criticism; the v1 low reading position is insufficiently justified.
- **Kokoro:** R182 supports rereading value and the importance of encountering the whole work, not only a school excerpt. Preserve the stronger reading-value case, while seeking scholarship beyond the existing specialist recommendation and student testimony.
- **The Doll:** R178 gives a work-specific formal and social argument missing from the earlier reader-heavy dossier, including an interpretive challenge to a simplistic heroine-as-villain reading. This strengthens inclusion and the case for moving it above candidates supported only by general selection pages.
- **War and Peace:** R180 supports the inseparability of historical argument and literary form. R181 records historical dissent, but its indirect, incomplete translation limits transfer to modern reading recommendations. Do not penalize the completed work based on a materially different reviewed text. A faithful English edition must preserve the complete novel; Bromfield’s early version is not an interchangeable shorter edition.
- **One Hundred Years of Solitude:** R183 strengthens formal complexity and interpretive openness beyond publisher acclaim. Do not equate a single Colombian novel with all Latin American experience; regional differences are part of the source’s own objection.

Method correction for the full release: difficulty is an entry-condition note, not an automatic downward adjustment in the reading-value order. Evaluate what sustained engagement offers. This addresses a weakness in the v1 Kant, Proust, Joyce and Mann placements without inventing numeric weights.
