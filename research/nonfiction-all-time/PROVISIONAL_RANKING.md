## What changes, and why

- Annihilation of Caste moves from 15 to 3: the recorded comparative praise for argumentative coherence receives greater importance.
- On the Origin of Species moves from 1 to 24: a historical scientific landmark need not be the highest-priority book for a reader seeking insight now.
- If This Is a Man moves from 18 to 2. That is an editorial hypothesis about testimony, supported by a particularly thin dossier that needs immediate strengthening.

These movements are hypotheses made explicit for review. The current evidence does not establish that adjacent candidates are distinguishable with this precision.

## Outstanding comparisons

This first shortlist still overrepresents philosophy and political thought. Expand science, history, economics, travel, memoir, art criticism and biography before calling the list all-subjects representative. Carson and Darwin now have direct supporting material; Levi, Douglass, Bloch and Trouillot need substantially more candidate-specific criticism. Philosophy can qualify as nonfiction, but must not crowd out the rest by default.

Before publication: expand the candidate pool, strengthen thin dossiers, verify specific complete English editions, review work/collection identities, and develop the owner-controlled criteria and weights. Keep these draft positions separate from every publisher’s original order and from personal overrides.
