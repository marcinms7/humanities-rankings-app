## What changes, and why

- Darwin moves from 3 to 22: this scenario gives direct contemporary reading experience more priority than the historical importance of a scientific argument.
- Middlemarch moves from 12 to 1, while Critique of Pure Reason moves from 8 to 25: engagement and preparation change priority without changing the underlying evidence.
- The Falling Sky moves from 24 to 14: this is a tentative case for widening intellectual encounter, with particularly incomplete book-level evidence.

These movements are hypotheses made explicit for review. The current evidence does not establish that adjacent candidates are distinguishable with this precision.

## Outstanding comparisons

Science, mathematics, economics, religion, art, biography and historical writing remain underrepresented. Bible/Qur’an/other composite scriptures, Euclid, Newton, Marx, Smith and major histories require explicit work-boundary decisions and fuller dossiers. Their absence is not a negative assessment. This comparison cannot yet establish a worldwide Top 25.
